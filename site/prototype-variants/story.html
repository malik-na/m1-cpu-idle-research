01 / An observation, and its limit

A pending command.
A precise limit.

On the M1, a Linux idle-path probe saw the APSC command’s BUSY bit set while three peer CPUs were inside their recorded idle callbacks.

The read came before the final barrier and WFI instruction. What happened at WFI—and whether a core physically slept—remains unmeasured.

04 / What this lets us say

Pending at the read.
Physical state still open.

The 18 witnesses support a pending APSC command at the instrumented read with all three peers inside recorded software callbacks. They establish neither BUSY continuity to WFI nor a physical power-state or energy benefit.

Issue #5 stays open. Further reboots are paused until a genuinely independent timing signal becomes available. Issue #6 turns to physical-state signal calibration.
